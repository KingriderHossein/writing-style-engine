# Initial Profile Cards

Schema version: 0.1.1

These profiles are **Experimental**. They are engineering hypotheses synthesized from professional guidance and the style-transfer literature. They have not completed the required 160-document media corpus validation or Persian perception validation. Do not describe them as validated.

## P01 — دقیق و خنثی

- Status: Experimental
- Goal: convey information with minimal affective pressure and explicit evidence boundaries.
- Typical fit: factual reporting, status updates, technical summaries, high-stakes contexts.
- Poor fit: storytelling that requires scene/character immersion; celebratory or community-building content.
- Style: short-to-medium sentences; low metaphor; low question use; low emotional markers; high attribution; low engagement pressure.
- Tone vector: formality 3, seriousness 4, respect 3, enthusiasm 0–1, emotional intensity 0–1, humor 0, directness 3, epistemic caution 3–4.
- Anti-patterns: hype, dramatic certainty, emotional adjectives that imply unsupported evaluation.

## P02 — روشن و توضیحی

- Status: Experimental
- Goal: make a concept understandable without flattening important nuance.
- Typical fit: education, explainers, help content, lay summaries.
- Style: moderate sentence length; high definitional/example/mechanism moves; controlled jargon with first-use explanation; clear paragraph topic structure; low-to-medium narrative density.
- Tone vector: formality 2, seriousness 3, respect 3, enthusiasm 1–2, directness 3, explanatory density 4, epistemic caution 2–3.
- Anti-patterns: unexplained jargon, analogy presented as literal mechanism, oversimplification that changes claim strength.

## P03 — راهنمای انسانی و مستقیم

- Status: Experimental
- Goal: help a reader complete a task with low friction while remaining respectful.
- Typical fit: product help, onboarding, service guidance, instructions.
- Style: action-first organization; second person when appropriate; active voice; short paragraphs; clear next step; low metaphor; minimal rhetorical questions.
- Tone vector: formality 1–2, seriousness 2–3, respect 4, enthusiasm 1, humor 0–1, directness 4, explanatory density 2–3.
- Anti-patterns: patronizing reassurance, forced friendliness, unnecessary “please”, vague calls to action.

## P04 — تحلیلی و محتاط

- Status: Experimental
- Goal: distinguish evidence, inference, uncertainty, and alternatives.
- Typical fit: research analysis, policy analysis, scientific interpretation, risk review.
- Style: explicit evidence/claim links; calibrated hedging; contrast and limitation moves; moderate-to-high information density; low emotional intensity.
- Tone vector: formality 3–4, seriousness 4, respect 3, enthusiasm 0–1, directness 2–3, epistemic caution 4, explanatory density 3.
- Anti-patterns: vague hedges, false balance, laundering speculation into fact, excessive caveats that obscure the main result.

## P05 — روایی-توضیحی

- Status: Experimental
- Goal: sustain attention by alternating concrete narrative material with contextual explanation.
- Typical fit: features, long-form explainers, selected science journalism.
- Style: higher scene/event/character markers; deliberate lede; explanatory “zoom out” moves; transitions between narrative and exposition; meaning/context statement may appear as a nut section rather than one fixed paragraph.
- Tone vector: formality 2, seriousness 2–4 by topic, emotional intensity 1–3, narrative density 4, explanatory density 3, metaphor 1–2.
- Anti-patterns: anecdote as evidence of prevalence, cinematic invention, bait-and-switch lede, delayed context that creates false implications.

## P06 — پرانرژی اما مهارشده

- Status: Experimental
- Goal: increase momentum and curiosity without overclaiming.
- Typical fit: selected social posts, launches, accessible educational promotion.
- Style: active verbs; tighter sentences; selective emphasis; moderate curiosity; low jargon; explicit payoff early enough to avoid clickbait.
- Tone vector: formality 1–2, seriousness 1–3, enthusiasm 3, emotional intensity 2, humor 0–2, directness 3, engagement pressure 3.
- Anti-patterns: false urgency, “you won’t believe”, hidden key fact, superlatives without evidence, emotional manipulation, certainty inflation.
